# Action Tracker privacy policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Action Tracker is a to-do app. This page explains what it reads, where that data goes, and how
to remove it.

## What the app reads

- **Gmail (read only, if you allow it)**: the subject, sender and short preview of recent inbox
  emails, and the full text of emails that look like a task, to suggest tasks and summarize them.
- **Google Calendar (read only, if you allow it)**: titles, descriptions and dates of events in
  the next 7 days, to suggest tasks.
- **Google Drive (app folder only, if you turn on backup)**: the app stores and reads its own
  encrypted backup file in a hidden folder. It cannot see your other Drive files.
- **Microphone (if you allow it)**: only while you hold the voice capture button, to turn your
  words into a task.
- **Photos and shared text**: only the image or text you choose to share with the app.

## Where your data goes

- Everything is processed on your device, including speech, text recognition and AI
  (Gemini Nano and ML Kit run on the phone).
- Tasks are stored in an encrypted database on your device.
- The only copy that leaves the device is the optional Drive backup, encrypted on the device
  with a passphrase only you know. Google and the developer cannot read it.
- No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for ads or analytics. The app has no
  servers of its own.
- Use of information received from Google APIs follows the
  [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy),
  including the Limited Use requirements.

## Your choices

- Switch Gmail or Calendar off under ⋮ → Permissions & features.
- Remove the app's Google access at Google Account → Security → Third-party apps.
- Turning backup off in the app stops new backups. To delete the existing backup, open Google
  Drive on the web → Settings → Manage apps → Action Tracker → Delete hidden app data.
- Uninstalling the app deletes all tasks stored on the device.
